Resources

The official places to check certificates, deposits, owners, agents and the law — every one free unless it says otherwise
These are the sources, not ours: each link opens the official site in a new tab. Nothing you type there comes back here, and nothing is tracked. What each one can and cannot tell you is written under it — a certificate that is not on any register has to come from the landlord.

Certificates and safety — what the landlord must hold

Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) — England & WalesFree. Type the postcode, pick the address, read the current EPC, its rating and expiry. A rented home must have one; from 2020 it must be band E or better unless exempt.www.gov.ukEPC register — ScotlandFree. The Scottish register, searched by postcode.www.scottishepcregister.org.ukGas Safe Register — check an engineer or businessFree. Check the registration number on your gas safety record (CP12). There is no public register of the certificates themselves — the landlord must give you a copy within 28 days of the check and before you move in.www.gassaferegister.co.ukElectrical safety (EICR) — NICEIC contractor checkFree. Confirms the electrician who signed your Electrical Installation Condition Report is registered. No public register of EICRs exists — the landlord must give you the report within 28 days.www.niceic.comNAPIT — registered installer searchFree. The other main registration body for electricians.search.napit.org.ukElectrical Safety First — tenants' rightsPlain-English guide to the 2020 electrical safety regulations for private rentals.www.electricalsafetyfirst.org.ukHow to Rent — the government guideFree. The landlord must give you the current version at the start of the tenancy; if they did not, a section 21 notice cannot be used.www.gov.uk

Where the data comes from

Every figure this site works out from a dataset comes from one of these, and each one links back to its own entry here. All of it is published by government or under an open licence, and the required attribution is shown with each. Nothing here is our data and nothing about you is added to it.
Local Housing Allowance ratesOGL v3.0
Department for Work and Pensions

The maximum housing benefit or Universal Credit housing element payable in each Broad Rental Market Area, by bedroom category. Rates from 2014 onwards; reset each April.

Source: Department for Work and Pensions, licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0gov.uk ↗
Price Index of Private RentsOGL v3.0
Office for National Statistics

Average private rents by local authority, monthly from January 2015, overall and by bedroom count. Used to show what rents in an area have actually done over time, and how that compares with the LHA rate.

Source: Office for National Statistics licensed under the Open Government Licence v.3.0ons.gov.uk ↗
National Statistics Postcode LookupOGL v3.0
Office for National Statistics · Ordnance Survey · Royal Mail

Every UK postcode with the local authority it sits in, and its coordinates. It is what turns a postcode you type into the right area for every other lookup here.

Contains OS data © Crown copyright and database right 2026
Contains Royal Mail data © Royal Mail copyright and database right 2026
Source: Office for National Statistics licensed under the Open Government Licence v.3.0geoportal.statistics.gov.uk ↗
Postcode to Broad Rental Market Area lookupOGL v3.0
Nick Bailey, Urban Big Data Centre, University of Glasgow

Links each postcode to its Broad Rental Market Area, which is the area Local Housing Allowance is set for. BRMA is not a standard statistical geography, so this lookup is what makes an LHA figure findable from a postcode.

Bailey, N. (2025) Postcode to Broad Rental Market Areas (BRMAs) Lookup v2.2. Urban Big Data Centre, University of Glasgow. doi:10.20394/mu6lw0x9data.ubdc.ac.uk ↗
