What Rent Costs Round Here

What private rents have actually done since 2015 · and how far housing allowance has fallen behind
Before you sign for somewhere, it is worth knowing what rents in that area have been doing — and if any of your rent is met by housing benefit or the Universal Credit housing element, whether the allowance has kept up. Both figures come from official statistics, and both are linked at the bottom so you can check them. Your postcode stays in your browser. It is used to work out which area file to fetch, and nothing about you is sent anywhere.
