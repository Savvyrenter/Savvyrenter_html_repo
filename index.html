Money

Interest owed to you

Worked the way a county court reads it: each overpayment joins the running balance on the day it was paid, and simple interest at 8% a year (County Courts Act 1984, s.69) is charged on that balance for the days until the next payment. A late first payment does not shift the rest — later payments are counted on the day the rent falls due.

Paying off arrears

A fair instalment plan when you have fallen behind on rent. If interest is added, you should only pay it on what is actually left — not the original lump sum. Put in what you owe and what you can pay each time, and it works the balance down to nil.

Redaction

Redact a statement page

A statement proves what you paid. It also carries your account number, your sort code, and every other payment you have ever made — none of which a landlord, an agent or an opponent is entitled to see. This blacks them out properly: the marks are burned into the picture, so there is nothing underneath to copy out again. Your file never leaves this computer.

Split a PDF into pages

A statement usually arrives as one PDF of several sheets. Everything else — redacting, naming, putting a bundle together — is easier one page at a time. This splits it: each page comes out as its own PDF, exactly as it was, with its number in the name. The file never leaves this computer.

Name statements by date

Statements arrive named things like statement_4471.pdf, which sorts into no useful order. This reads the dates on each one and gives it a name that starts with its period, so the folder sorts itself into date order. Your files never leave this computer.
