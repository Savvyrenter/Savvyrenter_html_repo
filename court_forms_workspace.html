Money Claim Online Workspace

Draft it here at your own pace · paste it into the real service when it is ready
DRAFTING SPACE An independent drafting space for people using the Money Claim Online service. Not connected to, or endorsed by, Money Claim Online, HM Courts & Tribunals Service or any court service. Nothing here is submitted anywhere.

Untested, unverified and possibly incomplete. This workspace has not been checked end to end against the live service. Treat everything it produces as a draft, and check it against the real form before you rely on it.

Found a step we have not covered? If the real service asks you something this page does not, send us that page — but save it before you type anything into it. An empty form has nothing personal in it to take out, which makes this safe and easy: press Ctrl+S (Cmd+S on a Mac), choose “Webpage, complete”, and then carry on and fill it in as normal. Do that at each new step, and when you are finished put the saved pages in a zip file and email them to us. If you only think of it after filling a page in, run it through our web page data scrubber first — it takes out your answers, your contact details and your claim number, shows you every change it makes, and never uploads the file anywhere. Worth doing even with a blank page: your claim number is usually in the web address at the top, which gets saved with it. We aim to have a missing step written and on the site within a couple of days, so the next person through gets an easier time of it — but this is a volunteer project run in whatever spare time there is, so that is an aim and not a promise. Real life very often gets in the way.
