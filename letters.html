Template letters

Free letters from Shelter, Citizens Advice and Which?, sorted by the problem
These letters are written and kept up to date by housing charities, so each link opens their page in a new tab. Read the advice around a letter before you send it: the rules are different in England, Wales and Scotland, and the charity’s page will say what has changed. Nothing you type there comes back here.
